Long documents hide a few answers. Ask for those first.

Paste a report, proposal or PDF into your AI assistant and ask for:
- the 5 key points
- every deadline and number
- what you must decide
- the risks
- a page number for each, so you can check

Always check the page numbers. AI can misread or miss things. For legal, tax or medical documents, ask a professional. Remove private details before you paste.

Save this for your next long document.
