Stop retyping tables by hand. Take a screenshot instead.

Upload a screenshot, or a photo of a receipt, invoice or whiteboard, to an AI assistant that can read images. Then use this prompt:
"Turn this image into a table with columns: Date, Description, Amount. Keep the original order. Mark anything you can't read clearly with [?]. Don't guess numbers."

Paste the result into your spreadsheet.

Always check the numbers against the original, because AI can misread digits. Don't upload IDs, bank details or private client data.
