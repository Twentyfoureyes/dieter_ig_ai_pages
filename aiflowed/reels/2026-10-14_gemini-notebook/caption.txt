Most chatbots answer from everything they've seen. Gemini Notebook (Google's tool, formerly NotebookLM) answers from the files you give it.

Add a few documents, then ask:
"Using only my sources: where do these 3 documents disagree? List each difference, which source says it, and what I should clarify with the client."

Great for proposals, contracts, reports and research notes. It can also turn the same files into summaries and audio overviews.

Still check key facts in the originals. Remove private details before you upload client files. Plans and limits change, so check what's free today.

Save this for your next client project.
