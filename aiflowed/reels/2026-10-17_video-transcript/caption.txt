Saved a long video you'll never watch? Skip to the good part.

1. Open the video description and tap "Show transcript"
2. Copy the text (on a computer it's easiest)
3. Paste it into your AI assistant with this prompt:

"Here is a video transcript. Give me the 5 key ideas, 3 things to try this week and any claims I should fact-check. Skip the intro and sponsor parts. Ask me if anything is unclear."

Not every video has a transcript, and auto-generated ones can contain mistakes. For very long videos, paste it in two parts. Check any claim that matters before you act on it.
