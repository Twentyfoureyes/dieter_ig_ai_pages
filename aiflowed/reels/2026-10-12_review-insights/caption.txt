Your customers already wrote your to-do list. You just have to read it all.

Collect 20 to 30 reviews, emails or support messages. Remove names and private details, then paste them into this prompt:

"Here are 30 customer reviews and messages. Find the top 5 complaints and the top 5 things people love. Quote one short example for each. Then tell me the 3 fixes that would matter most. Ask me if anything is unclear."

Fix the complaints. Use the praise in your marketing. AI can misread tone, so check the quotes against the originals.

Send this to someone who runs a business.
