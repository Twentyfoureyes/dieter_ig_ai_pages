Your inbox is not a to-do list. Stop answering emails in the order they arrive.

Part 2 of the email series: let AI sort first, then you write only what matters.

Paste your last 20 emails into this prompt:
"Here are my last 20 emails. Sort them into: Reply today, Reply this week, Delegate, Ignore. For each 'Reply today', give a one-line reason and a draft reply in my style. Ask me if anything is unclear."

Remove names, numbers and private details before you paste. Always read the draft before you send it.
