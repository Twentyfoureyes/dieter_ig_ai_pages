Researching competitors by hand takes forever. This workflow doesn't.

1. Pick 3 competitors your customers also look at
2. Ask an AI that can search the web to summarize what they sell, who they target, their selling points and customer complaints
3. Put all 3 in one table and look for gaps
4. Check the sources before you act. AI can be wrong.

Send this to someone who still does this by hand.
