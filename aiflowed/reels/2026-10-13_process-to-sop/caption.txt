If a process only exists in your head, you can't hand it off.

Fix it in 5 minutes:
1. Type or talk through how you do the task, step by step. Messy is fine.
2. Paste it into your AI with this prompt:
"Turn this into an SOP: purpose, numbered steps, tools used, common mistakes and a 5-item checklist. Ask me what's missing before you write."
3. Answer its questions.
4. Do the task once with the SOP and fix what's unclear.

Remove client names and private details before you paste. AI only knows what you tell it, so read the SOP before you hand it to anyone.
