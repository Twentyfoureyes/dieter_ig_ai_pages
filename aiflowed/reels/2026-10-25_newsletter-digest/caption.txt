20 newsletters, zero read. Let AI read them and send you one digest every Friday.

How it works:
1. Trigger: every Friday at 8:00
2. AI step: write a digest of the week's newsletters
3. Action: email it to yourself

Try this as the AI step:
"Here are this week's newsletters. Write a digest: 5 key ideas, 3 worth reading in full (and why), 1 idea I can use this week. Skip ads and repeats. Under 200 words."

No automation yet? Paste them in by hand once a week. Zapier, Make and similar tools can collect emails for you, but steps and plans differ per tool, so check what yours offers. AI can miss details: open the original before you act on anything.
