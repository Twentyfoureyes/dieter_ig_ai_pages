Chasing invoices is awkward. A good reminder doesn't have to be.

Three reminders, three tones:
- Day 1: friendly
- Day 7: clear
- Day 14: firm

Try this:
"Write 3 payment reminders for invoice [nr], due [date], now [X] days late: friendly, clear, firm. Max 80 words each. Include the invoice number and amount. No threats."

Check your payment terms and local rules, and read every email before you send it. Keep private details out of the prompt.

Save this for the next late invoice.
