AI sounds just as confident when it's wrong as when it's right.

Try this follow-up after any important answer:
"List every claim in your answer. Mark each one: sure, unsure or guessed. Then tell me what I should double-check and where to find a source."

You get a short check-list instead of blind trust. AI can misjudge its own certainty, so verify anything that matters before you publish or send it.
