Your AI forgets your business every time you open a new chat. Fix it once.

Create a Project, then add:
1. Instructions: who you serve, what you sell, your tone
2. A few files: your price list, a good past email, your FAQ
3. Start every chat for that work inside the Project

Try this as the project instruction:
"You help me run my [business]. My clients are [who]. Write in a [tone], short sentences. Use the files I added. If something is missing, ask me one question first."

ChatGPT and Claude both have Projects. File limits depend on your plan, so check what yours allows today. Don't upload IDs, bank details or private client data.
