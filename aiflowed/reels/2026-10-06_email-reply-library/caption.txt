Stop writing the same email twice. Build a reply library instead.

How:
1. Paste 3 of your best emails into your AI (remove names and private info first)
2. Ask: "Describe my writing style in 5 rules."
3. Save the result as your style prompt
4. Reuse it: style prompt + incoming email + "Reply in 3 sentences."

Always read the draft before you send it.
