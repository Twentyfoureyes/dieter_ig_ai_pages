Imagine your project already failed. Why?

Ask AI before you launch:
1. Describe your plan in 3 lines
2. Say it failed, and ask for the 5 most likely reasons
3. For each one: what can I fix now?
4. Pick the one to test first

Try this:
"It's 6 months from now and this plan failed. Here is the plan: [plan]. Give me the 5 most likely reasons, what I can do now about each, and which one I should test first."

AI only guesses from what you tell it. Use it to spot blind spots, then check with real people. Keep private data out of the prompt.
