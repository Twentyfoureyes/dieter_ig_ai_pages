Why ask the same thing every Monday? Ask once, and let ChatGPT run it for you.

Try this:
1. Open ChatGPT and describe the task and the time
2. Use this prompt: "Every Monday at 8:00, send me a short briefing on [my industry]: 5 news items, why each matters for my business, and 1 idea to act on this week. Under 150 words."
3. Check the first result and tweak the prompt

Good for briefings, weekly check-ins and reminders. A scheduled task only knows what you put in the prompt, unless you connect apps. Availability and limits depend on your plan, so check what yours includes today.
