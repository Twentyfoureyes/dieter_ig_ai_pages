Your first pricing call shouldn't be your practice run.

Ask AI to play the difficult client:
• It pushes back with 3 realistic objections
• It tells you what was weak in each answer
• It gives you a stronger line to use

Do it twice before the real call. Use made-up details, not real client data. AI is a sparring partner, not the client.

Send this to someone with a big call coming.
