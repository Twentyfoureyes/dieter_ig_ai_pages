An angry client email feels urgent. The reply you write in that moment is usually the one you regret.

Try this 5-step workflow before you hit send:
1. Find what they actually want
2. Check the facts
3. Draft 3 replies
4. Offer one concrete next step
5. Make it sound like you

AI helps you stay calm and clear. You make the decision. Remove names and private details before you paste. For contract or legal disputes, ask a professional.
