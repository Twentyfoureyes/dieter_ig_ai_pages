Rankings can't know your work. Your own tasks are a better test.

This 5-step check helps you decide before you pay for an AI tool:
1. Pick one real task
2. Run it with your real inputs
3. Compare it with a free alternative
4. Check privacy and export
5. Set a cancel reminder

A tool that tops a list can still lose on your task. Never upload private client data just to test a tool, and read the privacy settings first.
