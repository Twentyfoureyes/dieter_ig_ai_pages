Don't ask AI "Is my idea good?" It will almost always say yes.

Ask these 5 questions instead:
1. Who exactly would pay for this, and what do they use today?
2. What are the 5 most likely reasons this fails?
3. What is the cheapest way to test this in 7 days?
4. Which 10 questions can I ask real customers?
5. Can you turn this into a one-page offer?

You get risks, tests and a first offer in minutes. Then do the part AI can't: talk to 5 real potential customers.
