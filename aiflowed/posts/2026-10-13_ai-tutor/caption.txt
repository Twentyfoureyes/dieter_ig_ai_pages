Most people ask AI for answers. Try asking it to teach you instead.

Pick one skill you keep putting off: spreadsheets, ads, pricing, how to read a contract. Then swipe for 5 prompts:
1. Set your level and goal
2. Get a simple explanation
3. Get quizzed
4. Practice on a real task
5. Check what to verify

Start with 20 minutes a day. AI is a tutor, not an advisor: for legal, tax or financial decisions, ask a professional.
