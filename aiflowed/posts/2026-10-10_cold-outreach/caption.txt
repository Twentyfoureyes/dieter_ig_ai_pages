Cold messages rarely fail because of the offer. They fail because of the first line.

This 5-step workflow helps you write messages people actually answer:
1. Pick one client type
2. Find their real problem
3. Write a first line with one real detail
4. Keep it under 60 words
5. Plan one polite follow-up

AI writes the draft. You check the facts, make it sound like you, and only message people you can truly help.

Send this to a freelancer or founder friend who needs clients.
