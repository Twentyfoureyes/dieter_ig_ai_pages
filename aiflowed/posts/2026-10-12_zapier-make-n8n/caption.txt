Zapier, Make or n8n? The right one depends on you, not on the hype.

Swipe for a quick guide:
- Zapier: easiest to start
- Make: visual, more control
- n8n: most control, more setup

All three can run simple automations like "new lead > drafted reply". Start with one boring task, build it, then decide if you need more.

Plans change fast. At the time of writing, Zapier and Make have free plans and n8n is free to self-host (its cloud version is a trial). Check current plans before you pick.
