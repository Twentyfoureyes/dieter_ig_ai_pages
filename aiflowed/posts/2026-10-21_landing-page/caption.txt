Most landing pages fail before the first word. The writer never answered the five questions every visitor has.

Answer these 5 first, then let AI draft:
1. Who is it for?
2. What do they get?
3. Why should they believe you?
4. What holds them back?
5. What should they do next?

Give AI your real answers, then ask for 3 headline options. Never let it invent reviews, numbers or results. Read every line before you publish, and remove private client details before you paste.
