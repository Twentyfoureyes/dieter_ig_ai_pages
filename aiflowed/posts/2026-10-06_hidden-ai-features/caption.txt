Most people use ChatGPT and Claude like a blank search box. Then they wonder why every answer sounds generic.

5 features that fix that:
• Custom instructions: tell the AI how you work, once
• Projects: one workspace per client or job
• Memory: review it, delete what is outdated
• Search: find that great old answer in seconds
• File uploads: summaries, risks, task lists

Quick rule: instructions = what you write. Memory = what the AI writes. Projects = where it stays separate per job.

Feature names vary by app, so check your settings.
